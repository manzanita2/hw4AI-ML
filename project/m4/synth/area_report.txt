Logic cell breakdown by class (from 13-openroad-floorplan, pre-fill)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Class                              Count        Area (um^2)   % of cell area
  multi_input_combinational_cell   484,530       4,587,000      ~54 %  <-- dominant
  sequential_cell (flops)           96,607       2,494,630      ~30 %
  inverter                          60,714         227,896      ~3 %
  (buffers/taps/fill added later by PDN/CTS/resizer)

  Dominant area contributor: combinational logic (multi-input cells),
  4.59M um^2, ~54 % of placed std-cell area. This is the bf16
  multipliers (mul_bf16_p2, 8x8 mantissa multiply per PE) and the
  fp32 adders (add_fp32_p2) replicated across all 256 PEs plus the
  16 result-stage column adders. Sequential cells (the ~96.6k
  async-reset flops: PE act/psum pipeline chains, weight_store RAM,
  the two 257-bit AXIS FIFOs, result_buf) are the second contributor
  at ~30 %.
